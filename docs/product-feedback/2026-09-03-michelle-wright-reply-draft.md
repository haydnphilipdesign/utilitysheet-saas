## Draft

Subject: Re: Can info collected be edited?

Hi Michelle,

Yes, and here is exactly how far it goes, including where it currently stops.

You can change what sellers are asked. In **Settings > Seller Form**, under
**Form & packet type**, switching to **Advanced Utility Packet** opens up five
extra sections: Mailbox & Home Access, Security & Smart Devices, Lawn & Snow
Care, Irrigation & Watering, and Home Service Contacts. You can turn any section
on or off, and inside each one you can turn individual questions on or off. Those
become your defaults, and you can also adjust them per property when you create a
request.

Being straight with you about the limits, since your examples get at them
directly:

- **Garage door codes: yes.** There is a dedicated question for that in Mailbox
  & Home Access.
- **Other door codes: not yet.** No field for front door, keypad, smart lock,
  gate, or lockbox codes today.
- **Cameras: not yet.** There is a smart doorbell question, but nothing for
  cameras specifically. The Smart Home Notes field is the current workaround, and
  I know that is not the same thing.
- **Alarm systems: partly.** There is a security system question plus notes.

So you would be able to set up more than you can today, but not everything you
described. That gap is real and I would rather tell you than have you discover it
mid-file.

Separately, in case you also meant editing information after a seller submits it:
you can do that too. Submitted sheets are editable from your dashboard before you
share them, so you can fix or fill anything the seller got wrong.

Two questions, if you have a minute, because they genuinely affect what I build
next:

1. When you say short-term rental clients, are you coordinating the sale or
   purchase of these properties, or handling handoffs for properties that stay in
   operation? Those are quite different jobs and I want to make sure I am solving
   the right one.

2. What are the five to ten specific things you collect for these files today?
   Their exact labels, however you would write them. If there are questions we
   already ask that you would word differently, that is useful to know too.

You are the first person to raise this, so your answer carries real weight.

Thanks for writing in,
