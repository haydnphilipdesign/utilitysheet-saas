  treats that as spam. Space them out and vary the wording a little.

  these at signup). Change `utm_content` for each group:

  `lib/growth/attribution.ts`. Before linking from a new group, add its slug

  "facebook".

### Post 9: check the trash line (tip, no product)

- Groups: either
- Link: none

```
Small thing worth double checking on any utility list: trash.

"City pickup" isn't always right. Some areas use a private hauler the seller pays directly. In some neighborhoods the HOA handles trash through the dues, so the seller has never seen a trash bill and may not know who the hauler is. And bins often belong to the hauler or the town and stay with the address, which matters if the seller plans to take them.

Four questions cover it: public or private, who's the hauler, what's the pickup day, and do the bins stay.

The buyer will thank you on their first trash day.
```

### Post 10: closing moved, stop dates didn't (tip, no product)

- Groups: either
- Link: none. If anyone asks, the seller guide (`/guides/transfer-utilities-when-selling-a-house`) covers this.

```
Reminder for anyone with listings closing this winter:

If a seller already scheduled their utility stop dates and closing gets pushed, they need to call and move those dates too. Otherwise service can end while they still own the house, sometimes right before the final walkthrough.

And if the house will sit empty before closing in cold weather, remind them to keep the heat on to protect the pipes. Their homeowner's insurance may have rules about vacant homes too.

Easy fix: add one line to your "closing date changed" email. "If you've scheduled utility shut-offs, please move them to the new date."
```
