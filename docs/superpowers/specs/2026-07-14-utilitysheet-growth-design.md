# UtilitySheet Growth Design

**Date:** July 14, 2026

**Status:** Approved

**Horizon:** Initial 90-day validation cycle

## 1. Objective

Build a repeatable, low-cost acquisition and activation system that grows UtilitySheet from a small base of proven power users toward thousands of active accounts.

The strategy prioritizes active usage over raw registrations. The primary outcome is not an account creation; it is a transaction coordinator receiving a completed UtilitySheet from a real seller and then repeating that workflow.

## 2. Current Evidence and Constraints

### Product and audience

UtilitySheet gives transaction coordinators and real estate teams one reusable seller link for collecting utility information and producing a clean web sheet and PDF. The primary initial market is:

1. Independent transaction coordinators
2. Small transaction coordinator companies and teams

Individual agents and brokerage leadership remain secondary audiences until the TC motion is repeatable.

### Existing traction

- The May 4, 2026 auth export contains 120 signups.
- An estimated 10 to 30 accounts have used UtilitySheet on a real transaction.
- Several paying users use UtilitySheet multiple times to dozens of times per week.
- The strongest known acquisition sources are transaction coordinator Facebook groups and exposure to UtilitySheet on the other side of a closing.
- The existing site has named testimonials from working transaction coordinators and TC-company owners.

These signals establish that the product solves a recurring problem for the right users. The immediate constraint is distribution and activation, not basic product-market usefulness.

### Resources

- Founder-led execution
- Four to six hours per week as the initial operating assumption
- Less than $500 in cash spending over the first 90 days
- No broad paid advertising during the validation cycle

## 3. Strategic Choice

### Recommended model: TC power-user flywheel

The primary growth model combines the two channels already associated with strong users:

1. Targeted participation in TC communities
2. Product exposure across real closing transactions

Founder-assisted activation connects discovery to actual usage. Team expansion increases the value of successful solo accounts. Partnerships and search content are layered in after the manual motion proves repeatable.

### Resource allocation

- 70%: power-user, community, and founder-assisted activation loop
- 20%: TC educator and community partnerships
- 10%: template-led search foundation
- 0%: broad paid advertising

### Approaches not selected as the primary motion

#### Partnerships first

TC academies, coaches, and communities could provide concentrated distribution, but partnerships take time and depend on another organization's attention. They are a valuable second layer rather than the initial engine.

#### SEO first

Searchable templates and checklists can compound over time, but search is unlikely to produce fast activation evidence. The first 90 days will establish a small search foundation without relying on it for near-term results.

## 4. Funnel and Definitions

UtilitySheet will measure users as a progression:

1. **Qualified signup:** A TC or TC-company employee creates an account.
2. **Setup milestone:** The user copies their reusable seller link.
3. **Activated account:** A real seller completes the account's first UtilitySheet.
4. **Habitual account:** The account receives at least three seller submissions within 30 days.
5. **Paying account:** The account upgrades after UtilitySheet enters its normal workflow.
6. **Advocate account:** The user refers another TC, invites teammates, or produces transaction exposure that leads to another signup.

### Primary metric

**Number of accounts receiving their first real seller submission each week.**

### Supporting metrics

- Qualified signup to seller-link copy rate
- Seller-link copy to first seller submission rate
- First submission to three submissions within 30 days
- Habitual account to paid conversion rate
- Completed sheets to referred visitors, signups, and activated accounts
- Solo account to team invitation and team adoption rate
- Founder hours and cash cost per activated account

## 5. Initial 90-Day Targets

Because the current activation funnel is incompletely measured, the targets use baseline and stretch ranges.

| Outcome | Baseline | Stretch |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Targeted new signups | 150 | 300 |
| Accounts with a first live seller submission | 45 | 100 |
| Habitual accounts | 20 | 50 |
| New paying accounts | 10 | 25 |
| Referral or team-expansion wins | 5 | 15 |

These are validation targets, not forecasts. Results will be judged by activated and habitual accounts rather than signup volume alone.

## 6. Core Growth Loops

### 6.1 TC community loop

Participate consistently in a small number of high-quality TC Facebook groups and adjacent communities.

The content mix includes:

- Workflow education about collecting and handing off utility information
- Before-and-after comparisons of manual forms or email threads and finished UtilitySheets
- Specific customer stories from active TCs
- Product improvements made from TC feedback
- Discussion prompts about existing TC processes

Approximately one in four original posts may directly promote UtilitySheet. Other posts must provide standalone value. Every campaign link uses a distinct source identifier so reporting can distinguish signups from activated accounts.

Community participation must follow each group's promotional rules. Direct messages are limited to people who request information, sign up, or otherwise establish relevant context; the strategy does not use unsolicited mass messaging.

### 6.2 Founder-assisted activation loop

Every promising TC signup receives a short, personal message focused on their existing workflow:

> What are you currently sending sellers for utility information? If you send me the template, I'll show you exactly where your UtilitySheet link fits.

The activation service helps the user insert their reusable link into an existing email, checklist, Dotloop, SkySlope, Brokermint, or similar process. It does not begin with a generic product tour.

Follow-up messages respond to specific lifecycle states:

- Signed up but did not copy the seller link
- Copied the link but has not received a submission
- Received the first seller submission
- Reached three or more submissions

The manual process should capture common questions and successful language for later automation.

### 6.3 Transaction-exposure loop

Completed UtilitySheets can reach TCs, agents, buyer representatives, and admins on the other side of a closing. The live share experience should provide a subtle, contextual path for those professionals:

> Are you coordinating the other side of this closing? Create your own seller utility link.

This path belongs on the live share page and free-plan output. It must not compromise the paid white-label promise. Each link preserves a source or referral identifier.

After an account has several successful submissions, UtilitySheet may prompt the user to refer another TC with an extended trial or product-credit reward. Product credit is preferred to cash during the validation phase.

### 6.4 Team-expansion loop

When a solo user reaches meaningful volume, the founder asks whether other people should share the workflow. Small TC companies receive a guided 30-day team pilot with branding, defaults, and members configured.

The team motion is based on live adoption rather than a conventional presentation. A successful pilot means multiple team members use UtilitySheet on real files.

### Combined loop

Community discovery leads to personal activation, which leads to a live transaction, visible output, referral or team expansion, and more transactions.

## 7. Flagship Acquisition Asset

Create a **TC Utility Handoff Kit** that remains useful without requiring signup.

The kit includes:

- Seller utility-request email template
- Short seller text-message template
- Utility handoff checklist
- Example completed buyer-ready sheet
- Instructions for adding a reusable UtilitySheet link to common TC workflows

UtilitySheet is positioned as the automated implementation of the free workflow. The kit supports community posts, founder outreach, partner workshops, and the initial search-content foundation.

## 8. Ninety-Day Operating Plan

### Weeks 1-2: Foundation

- Produce the TC Utility Handoff Kit.
- Turn three power-user experiences into concise case studies.
- Prepare personal activation messages for the defined lifecycle states.
- Add source and referral tracking needed to measure the funnel.
- Create a simple weekly growth dashboard.

### Weeks 3-6: Founder-led validation

Weekly cadence:

- Two useful TC-community posts
- Fifteen useful comments on relevant discussions
- Ten personalized signup follow-ups
- Two conversations with active or inactive users
- Five partnership introductions
- One customer story or workflow demonstration
- One weekly funnel review

The cadence is a starting operating assumption. If it exceeds the four-to-six-hour weekly budget, preserve user conversations, activation follow-ups, and the strongest community activity first.

### Weeks 7-12: Concentrate and expand

Every two weeks:

- Identify which post, group, testimonial, and referral source produces the most first submissions.
- Repurpose the strongest customer story and message.
- Stop activities that generate registrations without live usage.
- Invite habitual solo users into team pilots.
- Ask power users for one relevant introduction.
- Pursue at least one live demonstration or workshop with a TC educator or community.

## 9. Budget

The maximum 90-day cash budget is $500.

- $100-$150 for thank-you gifts tied to substantive interviews or case studies
- $100-$200 in product credits for successful referrals
- $0-$100 for lightweight design, scheduling, or email tooling only if required
- Remaining funds reserved for one measured experiment
- No spending on broad paid ads

Product credits are tracked as promotional value and must be attached to an observable referral or activation outcome.

## 10. Measurement and Experiment Design

### Weekly scoreboard

- Targeted signups by source
- Seller links copied
- Accounts with a first live seller submission
- Accounts reaching three submissions
- Paid conversions
- Team invitations and multi-member team adoption
- Referred visitors, signups, and activated accounts
- Cash cost and founder hours per activated account

### Experiment protocol

No more than two meaningful experiments run simultaneously. Each experiment has:

- A written hypothesis
- One primary metric
- A two-week evaluation window unless the required sample needs longer
- A minimum sample requirement chosen before launch
- A final decision: expand, revise, or stop

Initial activation hypothesis:

> Offering to insert UtilitySheet into a TC's existing seller-email or checklist workflow will raise the signup-to-first-submission rate to at least 35% among targeted signups receiving the offer.

Landing-page micro-optimizations remain lower priority while the activation funnel is the larger constraint.

### Data-quality and failure handling

- Reports separate targeted TC signups from unrelated or unclassifiable registrations.
- Missing attribution is reported as `unknown`; it is not assigned to the most recent campaign.
- A seller submission counts as activation only when it represents a live transaction, excluding demos and internal tests when identifiable.
- If an experiment does not reach its minimum sample, its result is inconclusive rather than a win or loss.
- Community tactics that create moderation issues, negative sentiment, or rule violations stop immediately even if they generate traffic.

## 11. Scaling Path

### Stage 1: 100 activated accounts

Prove that community acquisition plus founder-assisted activation reliably produces first live submissions and repeated usage.

### Stage 2: 500 activated accounts

Convert proven manual work into reusable distribution:

- Recorded onboarding
- Behavior-based lifecycle messages
- Customer referral system
- Repeatable community content
- Several educator or community partnerships
- Searchable template library

### Stage 3: 1,000 or more activated accounts

Scale channels that do not depend directly on founder time:

- Placement inside TC courses and resource libraries
- Partner webinars and affiliate relationships
- Measured referral exposure across completed transactions
- Team expansion
- Organic search from practical TC resources
- Instructions or integrations for common transaction platforms when demand supports them

Automation begins only after the corresponding manual motion consistently creates first live seller submissions.

## 13. Explicit Non-Goals for the First 90 Days

- Broad paid social or search campaigns
- Targeting all real estate professionals at once
- Measuring success primarily by registrations or website traffic
- Building a complex affiliate platform before manual referrals work
- Automating founder outreach before its message and trigger are validated
- Shipping unrelated product features under the label of growth

## 14. Review Criteria

At the end of 90 days, continue and scale the strategy if:

1. At least one repeatable source produces activated accounts.
2. Targeted signup-to-first-submission conversion materially improves from the estimated current baseline.
3. A meaningful share of activated accounts reaches habitual use.
4. Referrals or team expansion show measurable early contribution.
5. Founder time per activated account declines as the best motion becomes clearer.

If these conditions are not met, the review must identify whether the main failure is audience selection, acquisition message, first-use friction, recurring value, or measurement quality before beginning another growth cycle.
