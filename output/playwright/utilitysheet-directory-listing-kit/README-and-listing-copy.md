# UtilitySheet directory listing kit

Prepared October 21, 2026 for G2, Capterra, GetApp, and Software Advice.

## Recommended product description

UtilitySheet helps real estate transaction coordinators, agents, and teams collect seller utility information and prepare organized buyer handoffs. Share a reusable seller link or create a property-specific request. Sellers complete a guided, mobile-friendly form without creating an account, confirming their utility providers and adding relevant property details. Address-based provider suggestions help sellers get started where available.

The dashboard tracks requests and submissions, and completed information is available as a shareable web sheet and downloadable PDF. Pro and Teams plans add custom branding, editing after submission, and Property Handoff Packet mode for collecting home systems, access details, and service contacts alongside utilities. Teams also includes a shared workspace and member roles.

UtilitySheet is part of Norma and is designed for the utility-information handoff during a real estate transaction. A free plan is available, with paid plans for individuals and teams.
